CHECKPOINT 3 CRITERIA — WHERE THE ANSWER LIVES
-----------------------------------------------
(a) Does every requirement belong to a Unit?
    Yes. Exhaustive 23-row mapping in units.md and on STEP-10-units.png.
(b) Are Units capability-oriented?
    Yes. Rules applied: own failure mode, own change rate, own external
    contract. Two capabilities were deliberately NOT made units, with the
    reasoning recorded.
(c) Are dependencies understandable?
    Yes. Three leaf units; one narrow 001<->002 cycle, documented and
    resolved by a single cross-unit call owned by 002.
(d) Is the Weighing Station treated as more than a screen?
    Yes, and it is demonstrated rather than asserted. Unit 003 carries 11 of
    23 FRs plus the whole state machine; unit 007 carries none. The session
    API is provable without a UI.
(e) Are integrations separated appropriately?
    Yes. Units 004 and 005 are the two external seams, each a leaf, each
    owning its contract's uncertainty in isolation. The scanner and printer
    were folded into 002 and 006 because neither has logic of its own.

THREE JUDGEMENT CALLS FLAGGED FOR THE REVIEWER
-----------------------------------------------
1. Unit 003 carries 11 FRs and is large. Splitting the session from the
   tolerance engine was considered and rejected (see units.md), but a
   reviewer preferring smaller units may want to revisit it.
2. Unit 008 has no FR of its own. Created because NFR-12 (supervisor
   authentication) and Q-08 (identity source) both point at one boundary. A
   reviewer preferring strict FR-per-unit discipline may prefer to fold it
   into unit 003.
3. Ticket and audit trail share unit 006. Grouped because both are
   supporting capabilities with a different owner and change rate. They are
   separable without difficulty — they share no state.
